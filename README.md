# AI Multi-Agent Financial Research Platform v2.2

![AI Multi-Agent Financial Research Platform v2.2](YOUR_V2_2_IMAGE_FILENAME.png)

## Overview

AI Multi-Agent Financial Research Platform v2.2 is an advanced financial research and decision-intelligence system combining autonomous specialist agents, quantitative market analysis, adaptive weighting, risk controls, scenario stress testing and a real GPT-5.6 research layer.

The goal of the project is not simply to generate market predictions, but to build a transparent multi-agent architecture where specialist opinions, confidence levels, disagreements, risk conditions and final decisions can be inspected and evaluated.

## Multi-Agent Architecture

The platform integrates 9 specialist agents:

1. Macro Agent
2. Stock Market Agent
3. Crypto Agent
4. On-Chain Agent
5. Derivatives Agent
6. Technical Analysis Agent
7. News & Sentiment Agent
8. Geopolitical Agent
9. Risk Agent

Each specialist analyzes a different part of the financial environment and produces structured signals, confidence scores, supporting evidence and risk observations.

A central orchestrator combines the specialist outputs into a unified market assessment.

## Consensus & Decision Engine

The orchestration layer evaluates:

- Agent signals
- Confidence scores
- Market score
- Consensus strength
- Agent disagreements
- Risk conditions
- Adaptive agent weights

Instead of relying on a single model prediction, the system evaluates multiple independent perspectives before producing a final research decision.

Possible final states include:

- BUY
- HOLD
- AVOID

The Risk Agent operates as a dedicated protection layer and can override the normal consensus when critical risk conditions are detected.

## Adaptive Agent Weighting

Version 2.2 includes adaptive weighting logic designed to allow specialist influence to evolve as historical evaluation data grows.

The architecture tracks agent performance and supports future dynamic adjustment of specialist weights rather than permanently assigning every agent the same influence.

This creates the foundation for a system that can gradually become more evidence-driven through measurable historical performance.

## Scenario & Stress Testing Engine

A major feature introduced in v2.2 is the Scenario & Stress Testing Engine.

The platform evaluates seven predefined hypothetical market shocks:

- Fed +50 bps
- BTC -15%
- Oil +20%
- VIX > 35
- USD +5%
- War Escalation
- Liquidity Shock

Each scenario measures how the multi-agent system and risk layer react under stressed market conditions.

The engine evaluates:

- Risk impact
- Market score deterioration
- Bearish agent participation
- Portfolio impact
- Final stressed decision
- Risk Veto activation

Critical scenarios can force an AVOID decision even when the normal market state remains HOLD.

This separates normal market analysis from hypothetical tail-risk analysis.

## GPT-5.6 Research Layer

The platform integrates a real GPT-5.6 research synthesis layer.

Instead of simply summarizing agent outputs, the LLM evaluates the combined multi-agent state, identifies conflicts, highlights uncertainty and converts structured system output into a professional research report.

The generated report contains seven sections:

1. Market Thesis
2. Bull Case
3. Bear Case
4. Uncertainty Analysis
5. Scenario & Stress Analysis
6. Executive Summary
7. Actionable Research Conclusion

The research layer explicitly distinguishes current market conditions from hypothetical stress scenarios.

## Risk Intelligence

Risk management is integrated directly into the decision architecture.

The system evaluates risk conditions independently from directional market signals.

This enables the platform to maintain a normal HOLD decision while simultaneously identifying scenarios where defensive positioning would become necessary.

The Risk Veto layer provides an additional safeguard against high-risk consensus failures.

## Research Integrity

A key design principle of the project is auditability.

The system preserves:

- Specialist reasoning
- Confidence scores
- Agent contributions
- Consensus information
- Conflict analysis
- Risk conditions
- Stress-test results
- Adaptive weighting information

This makes the decision process visible rather than hiding it behind a single AI-generated answer.
